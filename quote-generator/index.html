What to put on a quote

A clear quote wins the job and avoids arguments later. These are the parts clients look for.

Quote number and date
So you and your client can refer to the same version.
Valid until
How long the price holds. 14 or 30 days is common.
Itemised work
Each task or product with quantity and price.
Tax and discounts
Show them separately so the total is easy to check.
Terms
Deposit, payment schedule and what is not included.
Next step

Questions

What is the difference between a quote and an invoice?

A quote is the price you offer before the work starts. An invoice asks for payment after the client has agreed and the work or delivery is done. When a quote is accepted, change the title to Invoice and send it as your bill.

Is this quote generator free?

Yes. Creating, downloading and printing quotes is free with no limit and no watermark. A free account lets you save quotes and email them to clients.

How long should a quote be valid?

Most businesses use 14 or 30 days. Set the Valid until date on the quote so the client knows when the price may change.

Can I turn a quote into an invoice?

Yes. Open the quote, change the title at the top from Quotation to Invoice, update the dates and download or send it again.
