What to put on an estimate

Estimates are approximate, so explain what the price depends on.

Labour and materials
List them on separate lines so the client sees where the money goes.
Valid until
Prices for materials change. Say how long the estimate holds.
Assumptions
What you expect to find on the job, and what would change the price.
Tax
Add your tax or VAT rate so the total is realistic.
Deposit
If you need one before starting, say how much in the terms.
Contact details
So the client can accept or ask questions quickly.

Questions

What is the difference between an estimate and a quote?

An estimate is an approximate price that may change as the work goes on. A quote is a fixed price for the work described. Use whichever matches how you price the job.

Is the estimate maker free?

Yes. Make, download and print estimates for free, with no limit and no watermark.

Can I turn an estimate into an invoice?

Yes. When the job is done, change the title to Invoice, adjust the amounts to the final figures and send it.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Click Add your logo at the top of the estimate. It appears on the PDF too.
